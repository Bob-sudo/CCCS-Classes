All questions below refer to our standard mergesort.
For big-O,
your answer should be of the form $O(n^k \lg^\ell n)$ for
$k=0, 1, 2, ...$.
$\ell=0, 1, 2, ...$.
Of course $O(n^0)$ is $O(1)$.

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\nextq
For array $x = \{ 5, 3, 1, 4 \}$, how many mergesort function calls
are there when you perform mergesort on $x$?
\\
\ANSWER
\begin{answerlong}
7
\end{answerlong}

%------------------------------------------------------------------------------

in terms of $n$ where $n$ is the size of the array?
