The proof claims that every set of k horses have the same color. It removes one horse to obtain H1 and a different horse to obtain H2. The proof claims that because all horses in H1 are the same color and all horses in H2 are the same color, then all horses in H must have the same color.

The problem when k = 1:

When k = 1, the set H = k + 1 = 2 horses.

Let's say we have 2 horses:
Horse A - Brown
Horse B - White

We remove Horse A to obtain H1
{Horse B}

By the induction process, all horses in H1 have the same color

Now we remove Horse B to obtain H2
{Horse A}

Again, all horses in H2 have the same color

However, H1 and H2 have no horses in common. And the proof cannot conclude that the horses in H1 and H2 are the same.
