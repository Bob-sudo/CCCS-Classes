        Let $x, y$ be words in $\Sigma^*$ with $|x| = 0$.
We have the following:
\begin{align*}
              |x| &= 0                                                 \\
\THEREFORE      x &= \answerbox{ \ep }        & & \text{by Q1} \tag{a}     \\
\THEREFORE (xy)^R &= \left( \answerbox{ \ep } y \right)^R  & &                          \\
\THEREFORE (xy)^R &= y^R                  & & \text{by \answerbox{C4}}  \\
\THEREFORE (xy)^R &= y^R \ep              & & \text{by \answerbox{C7}}  \\
\THEREFORE (xy)^R &= y^R \answerbox{ \ep }^R  & & \text{by \answerbox{R1}}  \\
\THEREFORE (xy)^R &= \answerbox{y}^R \answerbox{x}^R & & \text{by (a)} 
\end{align*}
Hence $P(0)$ is true.
\qed
