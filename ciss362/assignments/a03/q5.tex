Let $x$ be a word in $\Sigma^*$. Then 

We have the following:
\begin{align*}
           |x| &= 0                                                       & & \tag{a} \\
\THEREFORE x &= \ep \\
\THEREFORE (x)^R &= \ep^R \\
\THEREFORE (x)^R &= \ep \\
\THEREFORE (x)^R &= x \\
\end{align*}

Hence, P(0) is true

Therefore, for some $x \in \Sigma^*$

\begin{align*}
           |x| &= 1                                                       & & \tag{b} \\
\THEREFORE |x| &= a
\THEREFORE (x)^R &= a^R \\
\THEREFORE (x)^R &= a \\
\THEREFORE (x)^R &= x \\
\end{align*}

Hence, P(1) is true

Therefore, for some $a, b \in \Sigma$ and $x'' \in \Sigma^*$, |x| &\geq 2

\begin{align*}
  Assume x is palindromic
\THEREFORE x = x^R

  And since |x| &\geq 2
           x = ax''b

           x^R = (ax''b)^R
\THEREFORE x^R = b^R(x'')^Ra^R
\THEREFORE x^R = b(x'')^Ra

Comparing the first and last characters in the string, we obtain
           a = b

Comparing the middle string gives us
           x'' = x''^R

\THEREFORE x'' is palindromic

Substituting b = a
           x = ax''a
Thus, (c) holds, with x' = a

Assume condition (c) holds, Then
           x = x'x''x'
where x' \in \Sigma and x'' is palindromic
Since x'' is palindromic
           x'' = x''^R
\THEREFORE x^R = (x'x''x')^R
\THRERFORE x^R = (x')^R(x'')^R(x')^R
\THEREFORE x^R = x'(x'')^Rx'
\THEREFORE x^R = x'x''x'
\THEREFORE x^R = x
           
  
\end{align*}
